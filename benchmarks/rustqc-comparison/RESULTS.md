## 7. Limitations

- Three workloads only (two 3-contig, one whole-genome rat); no human whole genome.
- One machine, shared hardware (load averages recorded per run in raw/).
- One RustQC version (v0.2.1).
- Upstream options with no RustQC equivalent: bam_stat: none; infer_experiment: -s/--sample-size has an equivalent; no U-only options; read_duplication: -u/--up-limit (plot-only y-cap, no R equivalent), --skip-plot/--rscript (R PDF execution; R always writes PNG/SVG+R and has no skip); read_distribution: none; junction_annotation: --skip-plot/--rscript (R PDF execution), --skip-bed/--skip-interact (R always writes both); junction_saturation: -l/-u/-s have equivalents (floor/ceiling/step); -v has equivalent (min-coverage); --skip-plot/--rscript have none; upstream has no seed flag (stochastic), R adds --junction-saturation-seed; inner_distance: --skip-plot/--rscript (R PDF execution; R always writes PNG/SVG+R); tin: -c/--minCov (default 10) and -n/--sample-size (default 100): no RustQC flags; -s/--subtract-background: no RustQC flag; --verbose: no RustQC equivalent
- junction_saturation is stochastic upstream (no seed) and reported as not-comparable, not as a pass or fail.
- tin P vs U differs by small numeric margins on rat (largest abs ~3.7, rel ~4%); recorded as differs at tolerance 0, including rows where P loses exact equality.
- This study is standalone (not protocol-v2); numbers must not be pooled with any other results directory.
